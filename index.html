In Australia, look for the logo — not just the word

"Organic" isn't legally protected for products sold in Australia unless they're certified. To know it's the real thing, look for:

ACO "Bud" logoNASAA OrganicColes OrganicWoolworths Macro Organic

Certification means an independent audit from farm to shelf. An uncertified "organic" claim can carry very little assurance.

The honest bit

This guide is a priority tool, not a health warning. The rankings come from US testing (EWG). Australia's own testing (FSANZ) finds residues on conventional produce are generally low and within safe limits — and dose matters. Conventional fruit & veg is far better than none. Use this to decide where organic is worth it, not to fear the regular aisle.
